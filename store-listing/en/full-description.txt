Prepare the topics common to almost every Spanish civil service exam (oposiciones), such as State Administrative Assistant, Administrative Officer, Guardia Civil, Police or Justice: the Spanish Constitution and Laws 39/2015 and 40/2015.

251 QUESTIONS IN THREE SYLLABUSES
• Spanish Constitution: 136 questions covering its 11 titles.
• Law 39/2015 on Common Administrative Procedure: 65 questions on interested parties, time limits, administrative silence, acts, nullity, procedure and appeals.
• Law 40/2015 on the Legal Regime of the Public Sector: 50 questions on competence, collegiate bodies, abstention, sanctioning powers, state liability and the organisation of the State Administration.
Each question includes the article that supports the answer. Questions are in Spanish, as in the official exams. Switch syllabus with one tap: progress, mistakes and best score are kept separately.

HOW TO STUDY
• Practice by topic: 10 questions, starting with the ones you have not got right yet.
• Instant marking with the explanation and the article.
• Quick test across the whole syllabus.
• Mistake review: go back to the questions you got wrong until you master them.
• Progress by topic so you know what is left.
• Streak of consecutive study days and your best score.
• Offline: study on the metro, on the train or anywhere.

MOCK EXAM
30 questions from the syllabus in 30 minutes, with no marking until the end and a third of a point taken off for each wrong answer, as in the Spanish State General Administration exams. When you finish you see your score, your net right answers and the explanation for each mistake.
