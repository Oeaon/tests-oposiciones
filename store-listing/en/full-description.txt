Prepare the Spanish Constitution topic, common to almost every Spanish civil service exam (oposiciones): State Administrative Assistant, Administrative Officer, Guardia Civil, Police, Justice and many more.

136 QUESTIONS COVERING ALL 11 TITLES
Multiple-choice questions across the whole constitutional text: Preliminary Title, fundamental rights and duties, the Crown, Parliament, the Government, the Judiciary, economy and finance, territorial organisation, the Constitutional Court and constitutional reform. Each question includes the article that supports the answer. Questions are in Spanish, as in the official exams.

HOW TO STUDY
• Practice by topic: 10 questions, starting with the ones you have not got right yet.
• Instant marking with the explanation and the article.
• Quick test across the whole syllabus.
• Mistake review: go back to the questions you got wrong until you master them.
• Progress by topic so you know what is left.
• Streak of consecutive study days and your best score.
• Offline: study on the metro, on the train or anywhere.

MOCK EXAM
30 questions from every topic in 30 minutes, with no marking until the end and a third of a point taken off for each wrong answer, as in the Spanish State General Administration exams. When you finish you see your score, your net right answers and the explanation for each mistake.
